# Control-M AI

**Section:** [[Control-M-AI-MOC]]

Control-M AI enables you to utilize AI capabilities and natural language to build, run, manage, and improve workflows. For more information about Control-M AI, see FAQ: Responsible Use of Generative and Agentic AI in Control-M.

Control-M AI includes the following tools:

- [[Jett-AI-Assistant]]: Enables you to prompt Jett in natural language, and then it searches the data in the Control-M database and utilizes AI to provide information to troubleshoot performance issues and generate custom performance reports.
- Control-M AI Execution Insights: Generates reports in Jett. Control-M AI Execution Insights gathers data from active jobs from the past four days, job change audits, and job action audits and utilizes AI to create reports for health checks, failure analysis, and performance monitoring.
- [[AI-Workflow-Creator]]: Creates a workflow with AI that consists of folders, sub-folders, and jobs in the Planning domain when you prompt AI Workflow Creator in natural language or attach an image.
- Control-M AI Pilot for Planning: Provides a list of actions and utilizes AI to create and configure jobs and folders in an existing workspace.
- Control-M AI Pilot for Monitoring: Utilizes AI to generate an analysis based on the job output, logs, and job definition.
- [[Control-M-MCP-Server|Control-M MCP Server]]: Connects Control-M to third-party AI clients, such as Claude Desktop and Cursor, to query job status, run jobs, and retrieve logs and output from Control-M.
